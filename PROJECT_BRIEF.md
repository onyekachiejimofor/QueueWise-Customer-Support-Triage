# QueueWise — One-page portfolio brief

**Challenge:** Improve the consistency of human triage decisions for customer-support messages where words can mislead department routing.

**Scope:** 240 synthetic support tickets, 8 balanced categories, 48 designed boundary/multi-intent cases, 96 greeting-variant pairs. Label Studio manual, one label per ticket.

**Work completed:** Annotated every ticket; aligned task IDs and full message text to source; compared first labels with supplied provisional reference; examined eight initial disagreements; preserved historical exports; detected and fixed a temporary invoice-category error; documented 10 representative hard cases and produced a routing playbook.

**Evidence:** 240/240 completed; 232/240 initial reference matches; 240/240 after reference-guided review; 96/96 repeat-pair agreement; 144 normalized distinct messages. These figures are integrity/concordance, **not blind accuracy**.

**Business application:** Draft routing rules, clearer ownership, and auditable labeling for a future human-reviewed pilot. Suggested evaluation measures include first-team assignment, avoidable transfers, and QA disagreement on new real tickets.

**Limitation:** No trained model, production deployment, independent blind review, measured time savings, or confirmed organizational routing policy.

**Demonstrated skills:** Text classification, intent analysis, ambiguous-case reasoning, Label Studio, QA auditing, data integrity, decision logs, business communication.
